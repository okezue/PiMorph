# Dataset hunt: confluent junction-stained monolayers with expert instance masks (2026-09-18)

Status: complete (single-day search, 2026-09-18).

Goal: a public dataset of a confluent cell monolayer imaged with a junction or membrane marker (ideally endothelial: VE-cadherin, PECAM-1/CD31, ZO-1, claudin-5, beta-catenin) shipping expert or manually curated per-cell instance masks, or closed cell contours that can be filled to instances. Non-endothelial confluent epithelia with junction stains and manual instance masks are second tier.

Previously checked and not re-reported as new: S-BIAD1540, S-BIAD1345, S-BIAD1169, S-BIAD1294, VE-Strat (Zenodo 13936923), mCellSeg (Zenodo 20174259), HAEC (Zenodo 4898011 / 4898134), Jacquemet Zenodo 13643590, 10611092, 13880404, 10617532, LIVECell, NeurIPS 2022 CellSeg, TissueNet, CTC, and the GitHub corneal set in `data/cornea_cells`.

## Downloaded and inspected (all under `data/`, not tracked by git)

### `data/hcec_ncam/` (Travers, Coulomb et al. 2025: cultured human corneal endothelial cells, NCAM + DAPI, manual segmentation)
- Source: `https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41598-025-14367-4/MediaObjects/41598_2025_14367_MOESM6_ESM.zip` (213 MB, HTTP 200) and `..._MOESM5_ESM.zip` (26 MB, tuned Cellpose "cyto3" weights, 4-fold model). Paper: PMC12379294. Licence: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (article licence; supplementary material carries the same).
- Content: `HCEC_NCAM_DAPI_Images_Label_ROI_Skel/` with 15 image sets named `L{culture}_{n}`: `initial_images_labels/img_label_*.tif` (ImageJ stack, 2 planes of 2048x2048 uint16: NCAM membrane channel and DAPI), `roi_images_labels/roi_label_*.npy` (2048x2048 int32 binary mask of the annotated region), `skel_images_labels/skel_label_*.png` (2048x2048 uint8 binary cell-border skeleton). Field 1330x1330 um (0.65 um/px). The paper states the 15 images were segmented manually in the Cellpose GUI and the masks exported as border images plus ROI.
- Inspection of `L0_1`: ROI covers 91% of the frame (2 components), the skeleton is 2 to 4 px thick (max half-thickness 2.24 px) and forms 4 connected components; filling `~skeleton & roi` with 4-connectivity gives 2,013 components, median area 1,524 px (p10 761, p90 2,904), only 56 fragments under 100 px. Cell pixels plus skeleton pixels account for 100% of the ROI (91.8% + 8.2%), so the annotated region is completely tiled and neighbours share a single border, no slivers. Instances are recovered by filling the closed skeleton (label 4-connectivity), the same route as the Alizarine set. A 512 px crop with the skeleton overlaid confirms the borders follow the NCAM signal and that every nucleus sits in one traced cell; the NCAM signal is patchy in places and the annotator closed borders through weak-signal regions.
- All 15 images: 1,177 to 2,509 cells per image with area >= 100 px (median 1,564), 25,056 cells in total; ROI covers 82 to 100% of the frame (mean 96%); cell pixels are 91.7 to 94.1% of each ROI, the remainder is the skeleton.
- Confluent: yes (primary hCEC cultures at confluence; "different cell culture quality" across the 15 images). Junction marker: NCAM is a lateral-membrane marker used here to outline cell borders; it is not a junction protein (VE-cadherin, ZO-1) but the images are fluorescence border stains of an endothelial monolayer. Endothelial: yes (corneal). Annotation kind: manual instance segmentation (Cellpose GUI), whole ROI.

### `data/alizarine_padova/` (Padova BioImLab "Endothelial Cell Alizarine" set, mirrored)
- Source: GitHub `adriankucharski/gan-synthetic-corneal-endothelium`, folder `extra_data/Alizarine/` (Kucharski and Fabijańska 2023, Biomed. Signal Process. Control 85:104985). Original host `bioimlab.dei.unipd.it` is NXDOMAIN. Raw GitHub download HTTP 200, token-free; 7.6 MB total. Terms: non-commercial research with citation (repo README); original Padova terms were the same.
- Content: 30 porcine **corneal endothelium** images, alizarine red stained, phase contrast 200x, 576x768 uint8 PNG (`images/`), plus `gt/` (binary 1-px cell-edge skeleton, 0/255), `roi/` (binary mask of the expertly annotated region), `markers/` (one binary blob per cell), `folds.json` (cross-validation folds).
- Inspection with `.venv/bin/python`: image 1 edge mask is a single connected 1-px-wide skeleton (max half-thickness 1.0 px); filling `~edge & roi` with 4-connectivity gives 283 components, exactly equal to the 283 marker blobs, median area 281 px (min 153, max 613). Inside the ROI, cell pixels are 89.6% and edge pixels 10.4%, i.e. the annotated region is tiled completely; neighbours are separated by exactly one edge pixel, no slivers. Across the 30 images: 251 to 481 cells per image (median 328), 10,257 cells total, ROI covers on average 27.8% of the frame.
- Confluent: yes (intact endothelial monolayer, in situ). Junction marker: no (alizarine stains the intercellular borders, transmitted light, not a fluorescent junction protein). Endothelial: yes (corneal).

### `data/rpe_nei/` (NIH-NEI RPE Mask R-CNN training demo set)
- Source: `https://github.com/NIH-NEI/RPE_Segmentation/releases/download/training/RPE_Training_Actin_RGB.zip`, 367 MB, HTTP 200 token-free. Full set (`RpeMapTrainingData.zip`, 4.5 GB) on figshare+ 28832501, CC0. Bharti lab (Ortolan et al. 2022 PNAS; RPE Pipeline). README states the GitHub zip is a demo subset; the full training data is on figshare.
- Content: 263 RGB TIFF tiles (768x768x3 uint8 composites; visually nuclei render red and the actin/ZO-1 cell-border channel green with a blue counterstain, channel means 53/47/93) from 3 confocal stacks of iPSC-derived **retinal pigment epithelium** monolayers (`P1-W1-TOM_E02_F001`, `P1-W2-LAMP1_B02_F001`, `P1-W3-SEC_G07_F001`), with one VIA-format JSON per stack containing polygon cell outlines with attributes `cell` (id) and `frame` (z).
- Inspection: stack `P1-W1-TOM_E02_F001` has 88 annotated tiles over 25 z-frames, 4,046 polygons, 486 distinct cell ids, each cell annotated on a median of 6 z-frames. Densest single tile has 98 polygons covering 63.8% of the tile with zero polygon overlap and zero direct label-to-label pixel contacts (neighbouring polygons leave background slivers). Per-z annotations are partial (only in-focus cells), so a 2D instance map needs a per-cell union across z. Doing that union per tile gives 152 to 251 cells per 768x768 tile, coverage 82 to 87%, with 10,000 to 13,000 direct label-to-label pixel contacts per tile (neighbours touch after merging; later z polygons overwrite earlier ones where they overlap), so the merged map is a near-tiling instance segmentation of a confluent junction-stained monolayer.
- Confluent: yes (mature RPE monolayer). Junction marker: yes (ZO-1 or phalloidin border channel per filename `ZO1`/`TOM`, RGB composite with nuclei). Endothelial: no (epithelium). Annotation kind: manual polygons (VIA), used as Mask R-CNN ground truth.

### `data/rescu_nets/` (S-BIAD1410 ReSCU-Nets, one image and mask pair)
- Source: BioImage Archive S-BIAD1410, `epidermal_cells/epidermal_cells_train/epidermal_cells_20240417_e3/` image and `_mask.tif`, 18.4 MB each, HTTPS FTP mirror HTTP 200 token-free, CC0. 15 time-lapse movies in total (394 MB images + 394 MB masks), split train/test.
- Content: Drosophila stage 14 embryo epidermis, E-cadherin:GFP or E-cadherin:tdTomato, 35 frames of 512x512 uint16; mask is uint16 label stack (35x512x512), annotation method per metadata: "watershed followed by manual correction in PyJAMAS" (expert).
- Inspection: frame 0 has 45 labelled cells (median area 1,729 px, min 1,019, max 3,206), labels cover 29.8% of the frame; neighbouring labels are separated by 1-px background seams (0 direct label-label transitions, 2,506 seam pixels). Annotated cells form a band around the wound (bbox y 107 to 396, x 9 to 506); after a 1-px dilation coverage inside that bbox is 61%, so the field is only partially annotated (wound region and far cells unlabelled). 44 to 47 cells per frame across the movie.
- Confluent: yes (embryonic epithelium), Junction marker: yes (E-cadherin). Endothelial: no. Annotation kind: expert-corrected instance masks, partial field.

### `data/flywing_denoiseg/` (FlyWing, DenoiSeg release of the Funke et al. 2018 epithelial tracking benchmark)
- Source: Zenodo 5156991 (`Flywing_n0.zip`, 49.2 MB, HTTP 200 token-free, CC BY 4.0). Underlying data: Funke, Mais, Champion, Dye, Kainmueller, "A benchmark for epithelial cell tracking" (ECCV-W 2018): Drosophila wing imaginal disc epithelia (proper disc and peripodial layers), E-cadherin:GFP, ground truth from Tissue Analyzer followed by manual correction.

- Inspection: test image 0 has 833 labelled cells covering 91.4% of the frame, median area 278 px (max 709, some 1-px fragments present); neighbouring labels never touch directly (0 label-to-label pixel transitions), they are separated by a 1-px background skeleton (14,997 label-to-background transitions). Across the 42 test images: 408 to 864 cells per image (median 781), mean coverage 91.6%, roughly 32,000 labelled cells.
- Confluent: yes (whole field tiled). Junction marker: yes (E-cadherin:GFP). Endothelial: no. Annotation kind: Tissue Analyzer + manual correction (curated instance masks, full field).

## Verdict

1. **A confluent endothelial monolayer stained for a junction protein (VE-cadherin, PECAM-1, ZO-1, claudin-5, beta-catenin) with public expert per-cell instance masks does not exist**, as far as Zenodo, BioImage Archive, IDR, Figshare, Dryad, Hugging Face, Kaggle, Mendeley, GitHub and the literature reachable today show. Every VE-cadherin or PECAM monolayer deposit found (S-BIAD1540, S-BIAD1345, S-BIAD1169, S-BIAD928, S-BIAD4061, Zenodo 10611092, 13936923, 13880404) is images only, patches, or model output; the annotated HUVEC sets that do exist (Chesnais et al. 2022 ECPT, Sierra et al. 2023, Vigueras-Guillén Rotterdam) are on request or offline.

2. **The closest thing to first tier is new**: the Travers, Coulomb et al. 2025 hCEC set in `data/hcec_ncam/`. It is endothelial (human corneal endothelium in primary culture), confluent, a fluorescent membrane border stain (NCAM) with a nuclear channel, 15 fields of 2048x2048 with roughly 25,000 manually traced cells whose borders close and tile the annotated region. The marker is NCAM rather than a junction protein, and the licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (fine for evaluation, not for redistribution of derived masks). It is the first real-data set in the project where endothelial instance ground truth at confluence exists with an independent nuclear channel, so it should replace HAEC (sub-confluent) as the primary endothelial accuracy benchmark.

3. **Best junction-stained substitutes** (second tier): the NIH-NEI RPE set (`data/rpe_nei/`, ZO-1/actin borders on a confluent RPE monolayer, manual VIA polygons; needs per-cell union over z to build a 2D instance map; full 4.5 GB set on figshare+ under CC0), and FlyWing (`data/flywing_denoiseg/`, E-cadherin:GFP Drosophila epithelium, 42 fully tiled 512x512 fields under CC BY 4.0). EpiCure and ReSCU-Nets add curated E-cadherin movies but with partial-field annotation in some movies.

4. **Corneal endothelium in situ**: the Padova Alizarine set survives only as a GitHub mirror (`data/alizarine_padova/`); its 30 images with expert closed contours are transmitted-light, not fluorescence, but they are the only public expert-drawn polygonal endothelial monolayer with exact tiling and are useful for vertex and adjacency metrics.

## Notes for use

- Instances for hCEC and Alizarine are obtained with `skimage.measure.label(~skeleton & roi, connectivity=1)`; drop components under 100 px (hCEC) or use the provided `markers/` (Alizarine) to reject non-cell fragments. Cells touching the ROI boundary are clipped and should be excluded from area statistics.
- RPE VIA JSON: keys are `filename+size`; each region has `shape_attributes.all_points_x/y` and `region_attributes.cell` (id) and `frame` (z). Tile index is the last dash-separated token of the filename.
- FlyWing and EpiCure labels have 1-px background between neighbours; the project's existing sub-12 px seam filling rule applies before vertex metrics.
- Nothing under `src/`, `tests/`, `runs/` or the other docs was modified; no git commands were run. Downloads live only under `data/` (untracked).
